Road Traffic Act Article 13 Paragraph 3. The driver of a vehicle must travel on the right portion of the road (on a road where the sidewalk and roadway are separated, the roadway) with respect to its center (where a center line is installed, the center line; the same applies hereinafter). Paragraph 4. Notwithstanding Paragraph 3, the driver of a vehicle may travel on the center or left portion of the road in any of the following cases: 1. When the road is one-way. 2. When it is impossible to travel on the right portion of the road due to road damage, road construction, or other obstacles. 3. When overtaking another vehicle on a road where the width of the right portion is less than 6 meters. However, this does not apply in any of the following cases: (a) When the left portion of the road cannot be checked; (b) When there is a risk of obstructing traffic from the opposite direction; (c) When overtaking is prohibited or restricted by a traffic sign, etc. 4. When the width of the right portion of the road is insufficient for the passage of the vehicle. 5. When traveling in accordance with the designation of the section and method of passage designated by the Commissioner of the City/Provincial Police Agency who deems it necessary to prevent traffic danger at a curve on a steep hill. A farm-machinery operator is not a person who can give hand signals. When traveling on the road, drivers of trucks, special vehicles, or farm machinery moving at slow speed sometimes gesture to mean "just go ahead and pass," but this gesture is not a hand signal.

However, this does not apply to roads where separate passage lanes are established. (2) When vehicles other than emergency vehicles proceed facing each other on a narrow road, the vehicle indicated in the following classifications must pull over to the right edge of the road and yield the way: 1. When vehicles proceed facing each other on a narrow uphill road, the ascending vehicle. 2. When, on a narrow road other than a sloped narrow road, a vehicle carrying people or loaded with goods and a vehicle with no passengers and no load proceed facing each other, the vehicle with no passengers and no load.
